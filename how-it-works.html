📦 End-to-End Order Pipeline

From your brief to your door.
6 steps. Fully owner-managed.

No factory visits. No compliance headaches. One WhatsApp message starts the whole process — and AvantGroups handles the rest.

The 6-Step Pipeline

Every order follows the exact same path.

No shortcuts, no surprises — predictable execution every time.

1
Step 1 — Send Your Brief
WhatsApp / Website Form
Buyer shares product type, quantity, specs, deadline, and budget. No minimum interaction cost. 98% of Indian B2B buyers prefer WhatsApp. AvantGroups responds within 2 hours on business days.
2
Step 2 — Quote Issued in 24 Hours
Airtable + PDF Quote System
💰 50% Advance Collected
Pre-built margin model: factory cost + 30–40% AG spread + logistics. Quote PDF generated and shared via WhatsApp. 50% advance invoice attached. No surprise fees, no hidden charges.
3
Step 3 — Raw Material Sourcing
Central Bulk Purchase from Primary Mills
Buyer's 50% advance funds the fabric or material purchase from tier-1 Indian mills (Surat synthetics, Ludhiana yarns, Rajkot metals). Pooled orders across clients = best volume rates passed to buyer.
4
Step 4 — Vetted Factory Allocated
660+ MSA-Signed Partner Network
Best-matched MSA-signed partner factory is allocated based on SKU type, idle production capacity, and QC track record. Buyer needs zero factory contact — AvantGroups is the single point of accountability.
5
Step 5 — In-Line Quality Control
Geo-tagged Photos + AQL 2.5 Sampling
Production milestones tracked via geo-tagged WhatsApp photos at 30% and 90% completion. Final random AQL 2.5 sampling before packing. Any defects = rework at factory cost, not buyer cost.
6
Step 6 — Dispatch & Final Invoice
Shiprocket / Cargo / International Freight
💰 Balance Collected
Order ships under AvantGroups dispatch label. Tracking number shared instantly. Balance (50%) invoice raised on dispatch. Factory paid within 7 business days of buyer acceptance. Dispute window: 48 hours.
