🏢 About AvantGroups

Built bootstrapped.
Zero VC. Full owner control.

India's first self-funding Manufacturing-as-a-Service platform — connecting buyers and 63M idle Indian MSMEs, with no external capital, no debt, and no dilution.

The Mission

Make Indian manufacturing accessible to everyone.

India has 63 million MSMEs operating at 50–70% idle capacity. Globally, buyers want India-made goods but can't navigate the fragmented, compliance-heavy supplier landscape.

AvantGroups is the intelligent middleware — the trusted face to buyers, and the reliable demand source to factories.

The result: Buyers get enterprise-grade quality with SME prices. Factories get steady export-quality orders without a sales team. The platform earns 25–45% gross margin on every order without owning a single machine.

The Origin Story

Why AvantGroups was built the way it was.

🔍 The Problem We Saw

Every platform in the MaaS space — Zetwerk, ZYOD, Fashinza, Groyyo — built something genuinely great. But every one of them spent crores on capex, hired 100+ people, and took VC money to get there.

We asked: what if you cherry-picked ONLY the operating insight from each — and skipped every capex trap?

💡 The Insight

Factori's 3-pillar model. Zetwerk's accountability layer. ZYOD's speed promise. Fashinza's compliance trust signal. Pietra's creator economy angle. Xometry's self-serve quote flow.

AvantGroups combines all six operating insights. Skips every capex mistake. Same market access, same margins, zero debt, zero dilution, full owner control.

⚙️ The Operating Model

Zero inventory. Zero factory lease. Zero machines owned. Revenue collected from buyers BEFORE paying suppliers — creating a structurally negative working capital cycle from the very first order.

📈 Year 1 Projection

₹80L+ platform GMV. ₹28L+ owner income. Break-even by Month 2. Total startup budget: ₹1.8–2.2L. No external funding required. Fully self-financed from Order 1.
