Factory Vetting — 5-Point MSA Checklist

To join, factories must clear all 5 checkpoints.

No exceptions. These 5 standards are what makes AvantGroups buyers trust us — and why we can charge a premium.

1
GST Registration
All invoicing must be under a valid GSTIN. No cash-only operations. Mandatory for AvantGroups single-invoice compliance and buyer audit requirements.
2
Sample Pass Test
Produce 5 samples on first engagement. Pass = MSA signed and first order routed. Fail = probation period with guidance. No exceptions on quality gate.
3
Capacity Declaration
Declare monthly production capacity, idle percentage, and lead time per SKU category. Must be updated quarterly. Transparency required for efficient order allocation.
4
White-Label Compliance
No AvantGroups branding may appear on shipments. Factories must ship under AG dispatch labels. NDA covers all buyer identity and spec confidentiality.
5
Payment Terms Acceptance
Accept 50% raw-material advance on order confirmation. Accept balance within 7 days of buyer delivery acceptance. Non-negotiable terms for all MSA-signed partners.
